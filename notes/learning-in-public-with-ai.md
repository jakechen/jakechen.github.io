### Inquiry
What is the purpose of a digital garden, and how does it fit a workflow where learning happens across multiple conversations with AI?

### Understanding
Unlike a traditional blog or a portfolio that showcases finished work, a digital garden is a living, interconnected graph that shows **how you think and learn**:

* **Growth shown through an evolving graph** — Increasing knowledge is demonstrated by an interconnected graph containing nodes at varying maturity stages (per [[note-maturity]]), joined by links or consolidated syntheses (per [[atomic-notes-and-mocs]]).
* **Open questions are valid** — Not every inquiry needs to become a settled stance right away or at all; tracking what is still untested is part of the process.
* **Two paths from `Alpha` to `Beta`:**
  * *Single thread validated:* If one `Alpha` note matures on its own without converging threads, promote and update it in place to `Beta` (relying on Git history or a short changelog for prior context).
  * *Multiple `Alpha`s converging:* When several `Alpha` notes build toward a consolidated view, leave the `Alpha` notes intact to preserve the original context and create a new `Beta` note that links back to them.

### Opinion
As an AI professional, most of my learning now happens inside AI conversations—and in practice, I usually have **multiple separate conversations on a topic (multiple `Alpha` notes)** before a consolidated opinion emerges as a `Beta` note.

Also, I hypothesize that structuring `Alpha` notes as **Inquiry → Understanding → Opinion** and preserving them when they converge into a `Beta` note works well because it separates *what the model explained* from *what I actually verified and believe*, without erasing the trail of how I arrived at the conclusion.

By showing how learning with AI works day to day—both where the tool accelerates understanding and where it falls short—I hope these distillations will help keep track of what works and what doesn't, for me and for the public.

*(This workflow is itself `Alpha`—as notes mature to `Beta` or `Prod`, the Inquiry/Understanding/Opinion scaffolding can fall away into a tighter concept note.)*
