Welcome to my digital garden.

This is a living, topological collection of working notes, mental models, systems architectures, and rabbit holes—written on my own or co-developed with an Agent.

Unlike a traditional reverse-chronological blog of finished essays, this space documents ideas as they evolve.

---

### Epistemic Maturity Lifecycle

Rather than plant-based metaphors, notes here follow the software release lifecycle based on validation rigor:

* 🟡 **`Alpha`** — Unverified hypothesis, mental model, or single-conversation takeaway. Complex ideas published early without waiting for a finished essay.
* 🔵 **`Beta`** — Bench-tested in code, hardware, or a live workflow, with edge cases still emerging.
* 🟢 **`Production`** — Validated across repeated runs with documented failure guards and cross-links.

See the full manifesto in [[note-maturity-alpha-beta-production|Note Maturity: Alpha, Beta, and Production]].
