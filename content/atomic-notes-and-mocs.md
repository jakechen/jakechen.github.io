### Inquiry
In a garden, do multiple seeds connect to help form a larger picture?

### Understanding
Individual exploratory threads stay small and modular, while broader worldviews emerge from how those pieces connect—independent of each page's [[note-maturity]].

Every page in the graph plays one of three structural roles:

* **`Atomic Notes`** — Self-contained pages capturing one mechanism, architectural pattern, or build log, distilled from raw conversations without back-and-forth noise.
* **`Bidirectional Links` (`[[wikilinks]]`)** — Inline links connecting notes sideways across unrelated domains whenever two projects share an underlying principle.
* **`Maps of Content (MOCs)`** — High-level synthesis hubs created once three or more atomic notes cluster around a theme, curating them into a cohesive thesis.

### Opinion
Extract one atomic note per distinct takeaway, link to other atomic notes in the same topic to help establish connections, and write a note of higher maturity stage (aka Beta or Prod) after multiple notes cluster naturally.
